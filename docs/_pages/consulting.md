# Consulting

The group undertakes consulting, advisory, and collaborative research projects involving statistics, data science, machine learning, forecasting, policy evaluation, and empirical analysis.

Our consulting work is motivated by real-world data problems where rigorous statistical thinking, careful modelling, and interpretable analysis can support better decision-making.

## Areas of expertise

The group can contribute to projects involving:

- Statistical and methodological review
- Data analysis and modelling
- Forecasting and prediction systems
- Evaluation of programmes and interventions
- Survey design and analysis
- Development of research reports and working papers
- Joint academic-industry research
- Case studies and executive education material
- Decision-support tools and dashboards
- Sports performance and valuation models

## Selected consulting and collaborative engagements

### National Foundation for Corporate Governance / Confederation of Indian Industry

We are collaborating on analysis related to the feedback survey and concurrent evaluation of the pilot project of the Prime Minister Internship Scheme in India.

### GDPS Innovations, Bengaluru

GDPS Innovations is a sports technology start-up working on AI/ML projects related to sports training and performance, primarily in cricket. The collaboration involves research papers and case studies on sports analytics and performance modelling.

### Real Estate Research Initiative, IIM Bangalore

In collaboration with IIMB-RERI, we work on various exciting challenges in the domain of real estate markets. One of the major projects in this domain was on innovative spatial modelling techniques that helps us create India's first scientific commercial rental index for understanding the dynamics of rental properties in various Indian cities.

### Smart City Mission, Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India

As part of the SAAR-Sameeksha series, we worked on evaluating whether the introduction of smart classrooms improved educational outcomes, especially in terms of reducing dropout rates among children from disadvantaged backgrounds.

### DBS Bank, Singapore

This project involved reviewing and evaluating a three-step methodology used for understanding the value of data in the banking industry. The work included theoretical and empirical assessment, followed by suggestions for improvement.

### SportsKPI, Bengaluru

This collaboration focused on analytics for kabaddi, including methods for player valuation and strategy-building during auctions and matches.

## Consulting philosophy

Our consulting approach emphasises:

- Clear problem formulation
- Transparent assumptions
- Rigorous statistical and machine learning methods
- Interpretability of results
- Reproducible analysis
- Practical recommendations
- Careful communication of uncertainty

We are especially interested in projects where methodological depth and real-world relevance can inform each other.
